Evaluation and analysis of multi-turn language and vision-language agents in long-horizon environments, focusing on planning, action coherence, temporal consistency, grounding, and reward-reliability decoupling.

## Motivation

Long-horizon agents must maintain coherent plans and grounded actions over many steps. Evaluating only final success can miss failures in reasoning, grounding, or temporal consistency.

## What I Worked On

- Multi-turn evaluation of LLM/VLM agents.
- Diagnostics for planning, action coherence, temporal consistency, and grounding.
- Analysis of how reward signals relate to reliable agent behavior.
