**TL;DR:** This work combines a VLM reward for progress over several steps with a positional reward to guide robot learning. Across six MetaWorld manipulation tasks, running average success reaches 78.2% versus 63.8% for the best-tuned positional baseline at the same training budget, with improvements in five tasks.
